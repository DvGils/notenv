# notenv threat model

The full threat model now lives in the documentation site:

**<https://dvgils.github.io/notenv/security/threat-model/>**

It states what notenv protects, against whom, and (just as importantly) the explicit non-goals and
known limitations: the assets, the adversaries, the properties that hold against each, and where
reality is weaker than the ideal.

To report a vulnerability, see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
